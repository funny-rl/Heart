# Code of conduct

## Our standard

HEART aims to provide a welcoming, rigorous environment for research and open
source collaboration. Participants are expected to be respectful, accept
constructive technical disagreement, credit others' work, distinguish evidence
from opinion, and focus criticism on ideas and implementations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, threats, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption,
plagiarism, or publishing another person's private information without consent.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, community spaces, and public situations where someone represents
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through a private contact
channel on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue. Reports will be reviewed in context and kept as confidential as
practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this
standard. Good-faith reporters must not face retaliation.
